💡 Comprehensive Guide to AI Background Remover

1. Is this free, and what are the limits?

The tool is free to use and accepts images up to 10MB each. Processing speed and availability depend on your device memory and browser. On first use, the model and one browser runtime total about 55MB.

2. Are my images safe?

The app does not upload the selected image to a processing server. Inference runs in your browser, and pinned AI code, model, and runtime assets are served by this site without third-party CDN requests. Browser extensions and the device itself remain outside this guarantee.

3. Why is the first download about 55MB?

To run without a remote image-processing API, the browser downloads a 44.3MB quantized model plus an approximately 10MB WASM runtime from this site. The browser may reuse its cache, but storage cleanup or eviction can require another download.
