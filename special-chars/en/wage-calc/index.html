💡 Wage Converter Guide & FAQ

📖 How to Use
  1. Enter any one of: hourly rate, monthly salary, or annual salary — the others auto-calculate.
  2. Adjust hours per day and days per week for accurate conversion.
  3. Select an illustrative deduction rate to compare gross and estimated net pay.
  4. Use the "How many hours" feature to see how long you'd need to work for any purchase.
⏰ How is overtime pay calculated?

Extra overtime hours are valued at 1.5× the regular rate. The optional premium field adds another 0.5× for hours that already form part of the regular schedule. Overtime and premium rules vary by jurisdiction and contract, so enter only the hours and multiplier that apply to you.

📅 How are weekly and monthly hours converted?

Scheduled weekly hours are hours per day × days per week. Monthly values use 52 weeks ÷ 12 months. The calculator does not add country-specific paid-weekly-holiday allowances automatically.

🧾 How does the tax deduction work?

The deduction selector is only a simple percentage estimate. It does not calculate tax brackets, credits, insurance, filing status, or local rules, and should not be treated as payroll or tax advice.
